## 4. What this version provides

**A new look, in black and white.** The owner's ask: change the whole
app's design and motion using the three references. His picks: dark
(Netflix + 1367), a monochrome accent, every surface in stages, and four
effects — Netflix hover cards, 1367 reveals and header pill, page
cross-fades, and Netflix's numbered Top 10.

- **Web pages** (Home, Discover, the six catalogues, Games/Apps/Websites,
  Saved, History, Schedule, Downloads): a full-width billboard with a
  "01 / 06" counter; cards that grow, take a white edge and lean toward
  the pointer; rows paged by arrow buttons; things rising into place as
  they are scrolled into, never on a redraw.
- **Discover** is Top 10 rows only — Anime, Series, Movies, Manga,
  Manhwa, Manhua, Other Readings — each title once, with outlined rank
  numerals; Latest Chapters and Reading rows are gone.
- **The window**: pages cross-fade; the search field and its three
  buttons draw into a capsule when a page scrolls, in full screen too.
- **The details page**, the **player's** seek strip and episode list,
  and **Settings** are redesigned to match; titles are in Bahnschrift,
  small labels in Cascadia Mono.
- **A new icon** — the owner's black tile with a white "A".

**Resume checks for a new chapter.** On a reading card, when the list on
disk has the reader on its newest chapter, Resume asks the site first
and opens whatever is next on the fresh list.

**Reading cards show the last chapter read**, not the next one.

**Back no longer types into the search field.** Leaving a details page
handed the keyboard to the title bar's field.

**"Hide them from Discover page too"** in Settings > Preferences.

## 5. Design notes

**Motion from the references' own styles.** The curves were read off
each site's computed styles: 1367's `(.16,1,.3,1)` for things arriving,
A24's `(.32,.72,0,1)` for things opening, Netflix's `(.32,.94,.6,1)` for
hover. Everything that moves on the web pages is opacity or transform.

**A web page cannot be grabbed, so the old page slide moved blank
ground.** `widgets.PageFade` reads the old page off the screen
(`QScreen.grabWindow(0)`, 25–26 ms) and paints it under a rising veil of
the ground (2.0–3.3 ms a frame). Its order matters: the picture goes up
before the new page is built, the new page stays hidden until the fade
lands, and the fade is repainted in the same call that hides the old
page. A layered native child (DWM alpha) was tried first and is never
drawn in this window. The page slide's vertical half and
`ANIM_DURATION_MS` were removed at the owner's word; the sidebar swap
keeps `PageSlide`.

**The details page's entrance and exit** are pictures, not the widgets:
`_Entrance` blits the backdrop and the two halves; the page is pictured
off screen (`WA_DontShowOnScreen`) because grabbing it after the real
show put a 58 ms frame of bare ground on screen. `_Exit` animates over a
screen capture of the page underneath, taken when the details page opens.

**The full-screen bar's click mask also clips its painting**, so the
capsule is included in the mask while it shows.

**Colours baked into files.** Letterboxed game tiles carried the old
navy panel; `game_art.repaint_posters` refills them at launch, and
`backend.local_url` puts a file's modification time in its image token
so the day-long cache cannot keep the old picture.

**Bahnschrift, not Sitka.** "Sitka Display" resolved to nothing in
Chromium (a Times fallback) and to a serif in Qt; Bahnschrift is a real
family on every Windows 10/11 machine.

## 10. Verification performed

**Frozen builds driven from outside on a copy of the owner's data**
(rule 12), photographed through each stage: the web pages (Home,
Discover, the catalogues, Games), the header capsule windowed and in
full screen, the details page with its entrance, and the player's bars.

**Measured**: Discover's first content 0.6 s warm, 0.8-0.9 s cold, the
same on the 2.13 build and on this one; glides 4-8 ms with a card
leaning under the pointer; page fades sampled at the panel's rate with
no ground frame in 8 + 6 switches; the details entrance with no ground
frame in 3 of 3 runs. Resume's chapter check 0.61-3.97 s over the
owner's eight titles, 1.97 s on the frozen build opening chapter 889.

**The release build**: `app.zip` 1,609 entries, version 3.0, the new
icon; `build.py`'s bundle verification passed.

**Defender**, `MpCmdRun -Scan -ScanType 3 -DisableRemediation`, service
Running: exit 0 on the app `Atomic.exe` (twice), the bridge
`Atomic.exe` and `Atomic.zip`.

**The release-notes gate:** seven notes written for 3.0.

**Not measured by me.** The last build's icon, Settings pointing-hand
cursor, rounded Save button and centred season arrows - the owner
tested that build himself, at his word; his laptop.
